Privacy & data

Dashers is developed by Soupper. For privacy questions or requests, contact S0upp3r@gmail.com.

Development notice

Dashers is not publicly released yet. This page describes this website and the current prototype. The final Android build and its third-party services are still being reviewed. An updated, release-specific privacy policy will be provided before public distribution.

This website

The site does not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts, account registration, contact forms, or site-managed cookies. Its images, styles and scripts are hosted with the site. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary request information, such as your IP address and browser information, when serving pages. See GitHub's privacy statement for its practices.

Local game preferences

The current game stores your chosen nickname, character selection and the last host address on your device to support the game menu and reconnecting. Game and audio settings may also be saved locally. You can change your nickname in the menu or use a backup name. Clearing the app's storage through Android settings removes its local preferences and resets the app.

Local multiplayer

Joining a match connects your device to a player-hosted game. Your nickname, selected character and gameplay information, including movement and scores, are sent as needed to the host and other connected players. A network connection also exposes network addressing information to the host. Choose a nickname rather than your real name and play with people you trust. The current game does not require a Soupper account or use a Soupper-hosted matchmaking service.

Advertising and third-party services

The current arenas show the game's own promotional signs. Paid billboard advertising is not connected. Advertising SDKs are present in the development project, but their final inclusion and data behavior have not been approved for release. Advertising, consent choices, SDK providers and any data disclosures will be documented for the actual release before paid advertising is enabled.

Children and families

Dashers is intended to be approachable for a broad audience. Child-appropriate advertising and privacy handling are part of the release review. Children should not use a real name as their game nickname or send personal information in support messages. A parent or guardian can contact Soupper with privacy concerns.

Support messages and deletion requests

If you email Soupper, your email address, message and any attachments are received to answer your request. Share only the information needed to explain the issue; do not send passwords, financial information or sensitive personal details. To request deletion of information you have sent through support, email the same address and identify the message or request. Information needed to fulfill legal obligations may need to be retained.

Local multiplayer information is also received by other players' devices. Soupper does not control copies or recordings made by other players.

Changes

This page will be revised as the game and its services change. The update date appears above. For questions about a specific build, include its version number when contacting support.
