Dashers privacy policy

Updated October 18, 2026. This policy describes Dashers and its configured Android advertising features. Dashers is currently in development and testing. Features available in a particular build may vary.

Dashers is developed by Soupper. Privacy and support contact: S0upp3r@gmail.com.

On-device information

Dashers stores your chosen nickname, character choice, game/audio settings, previous host address and cosmetic reward entitlement on your device. For optional advertising, it stores only an age band: unknown, under 18 or adult. Exact age entered for this choice is discarded and is not explicitly sent by the game. Clearing Dashers app storage in Android settings resets these preferences and locally unlocked effects.

Local multiplayer

The game connects directly to a player-hosted match on a local network. Your nickname, chosen character and gameplay information, including movement and scores, are sent to the host and connected players. Network connections expose addressing information to peers. Use a nickname instead of your real name, play with people you trust and use a trusted Wi-Fi network. Local gameplay traffic should not be assumed to be encrypted. Soupper does not provide a central game account, public matchmaking service or central gameplay database for this version. Other players can see or record information displayed to them.

Optional advertising and privacy choices

Children, teens and players of unknown age receive no ad requests from Dashers' ad controller. Skipping the optional age question allows continued play without ads. The game does not explicitly initialize its Google ad service until a player has supplied an adult age band and the required privacy flow permits requests.

Eligible adults may see an occasional interstitial after leaving completed matches, choose a cosmetic rewarded ad from the disconnected menu, or open an optional Sponsors page. Ads never grant a score or combat advantage. Paid in-world billboard ads are not part of this version. Privacy choices are available when required by Google's consent service.

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform provide ad/consent services. Depending on the feature, consent and SDK configuration, Google may collect or share information including IP-derived approximate location, identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google's services use their own retention practices. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information . Do not send Soupper device identifiers or other personal information to obtain an optional cosmetic reward.

Children and families

Dashers includes children in its intended audience. Children and users of unknown age can play without advertising. Children should use a non-personal nickname and should not share personal information with other players or in support messages. A parent or guardian may contact Soupper about privacy concerns. The game does not require children to create a game account or sign in to a social account.

Support, retention and deletion

If you email support, Soupper and the email service receive your address, message and attachments to answer the request. Share only what is necessary and avoid sensitive information. Request deletion of support correspondence at S0upp3r@gmail.com, identifying the request to be removed. Correspondence may be retained while needed to resolve a request or meet applicable obligations. Local preferences remain until changed, cleared or removed through your device; Soupper cannot delete another player's recordings or data independently retained by third-party services.

Website

The Dashers website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives ordinary web request information such as IP/browser details. The website contains no custom analytics, advertising scripts, registration forms or site-managed cookies. See GitHub Privacy Statement .

Changes

This notice will be updated when the game or its services change. Contact Soupper with the game version when asking about a specific build.
