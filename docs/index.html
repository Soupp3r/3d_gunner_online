COMING IN A FUTURE UPDATE

Keep moving.
The arena has plans.

The following features are in development and are not in the current Google Play release. Nearby-game discovery will make joining easier, and perfect blocks will briefly stun and push back an attacker.

A random event will appear during existing rounds, with a warning before it activates. Players are never teleported into events: choose your route and react to what is around you.

Pulse

Leave the marked circle or block. Contact briefly stuns and pushes outward without awarding points.

Snare

Walking slows only while you are inside the patch. Move out or dash clear.

Bounce pads

Contact gives an outward, collision-limited bounce, once per fighter per event.

Temporary walls

A wall changes your route for a few seconds. It waits for its footprint to clear before appearing.

Moving blocks

A block travels along a short track and pauses near fighters. Go around and find a new angle.

Contested boosts

Touch the target with an empty tonic slot to claim temporary speed. Only one fighter can collect it.

These features have passed local development checks. Android and two-device testing are still ahead. Same-Wi-Fi play and first-to-three rounds remain at the heart of Dashers.

Where can I download Dashers?

Dashers is available for Android on Google Play. Store availability depends on your country and device. Features marked as upcoming on this page are not included in the current release.

My friend can't join. What should we check?

Use the same Wi-Fi and game version. Enter the host device's local address, not 127.0.0.1. Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation can prevent devices from connecting. Make sure the host has started a game.

Can we play across the internet?

The current game uses direct local-network multiplayer. Public matchmaking and relay services are not included.

Does the game work on a TV?

On compatible Android phones and receivers, you may be able to mirror your phone screen using the phone's casting settings. This is screen mirroring, not a separate TV app or a separate controller display. Compatibility depends on your devices.

How do I change or remove my nickname?

Change the name in the game menu, or choose “Use backup name” to replace it. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings removes locally saved preferences and resets the app.
