Where can I download Dashers?

Dashers is in development. The Google Play release is being prepared; there is no public download available from this page yet.

My friend can't join. What should we check?

Use the same Wi-Fi and game version. Enter the host device's local address, not 127.0.0.1. Guest Wi-Fi or client isolation can prevent devices from connecting. Make sure the host has started a game.

Can we play across the internet?

The current game uses direct local-network multiplayer. Public matchmaking and relay services are not included.

Does the game work on a TV?

On compatible Android phones and receivers, you may be able to mirror your phone screen using the phone's casting settings. This is screen mirroring, not a separate TV app or a separate controller display. Compatibility depends on your devices.

How do I change or remove my nickname?

Change the name in the game menu, or choose “Use backup name” to replace it. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings removes locally saved preferences and resets the app.
